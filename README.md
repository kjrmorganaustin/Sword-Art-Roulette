# SAO ROULETTE

Tired of forgetting what the SAO subreddit link is? 
Just gamble away your fate at whether you find manga or anime spoilers or if you find game spoilers!

# About Me
Hey! 

I’m Kay, a software developer taking part in Hack Club’s hacklet (https://hacklet.hackclub.com) YSWS (You Ship We Ship)
This is a simple challenge for many developers. Build a bookmarklet without the use of AI, and ship it! Naturally, I wanted to combine my biggest interests. Programming is one of my favorite activities, and SAO is one of my favorite anime. So, I made a simple roulette that lets you gamble away whether you find SAO spoilers, or Alicization Lycoris spoilers! Either way, the whole franchise will be ruined for you!

# How To Use?

It’s simple! Just go into `bookmarklet.js` and copy the code. Create a new bookmark and paste the code in the URL bar! After that, click the URL! Have fun!
